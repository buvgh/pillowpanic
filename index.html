FAQ · 常见问题

How do I play with a friend? · 怎么和朋友一起玩？

Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi, open “LAN battle” on each, then one taps “Create room” and the other “Join room”. Allow “Local Network” when iOS asks.

两台设备连同一个 Wi-Fi，都打开“局域网对战”，一台点“创建房间”，另一台点“加入房间”。系统询问“本地网络”权限时请选择允许。

Where is my progress saved? · 进度保存在哪里？

On your device only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes its progress.

只保存在你的设备上，没有账号，也不会上传。删除 App 会同时删除进度。

How do I replay the tutorial? · 怎么重新玩新手教学？

Settings → Help → Replay tutorial. 设置 → 帮助 → 重新玩新手教学。

The game feels slow on my older device · 老机型不够流畅

Settings → Display: choose 60 or 30 FPS and the “Standard” or “Battery saver” resolution.

设置 → 画面：选择 60 帧或 30 帧，分辨率选“标准”或“省电”。
