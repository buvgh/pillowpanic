Pillow Panic — Privacy Policy

Updated: October 30, 2026

Pillow Panic is a paid-download game. The current version has no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, in-app purchases, or online account system. Apple handles App Store purchases and Google handles Google Play purchases; the game does not receive your payment-card details.

Data on your device

The game stores your chosen nickname, a locally generated profile identifier, characters, earned coins, progress, pet status, settings and match history on your device. Match events help the computer opponent adapt locally. Technical performance reports may also be saved locally. The game does not automatically upload these files to the developer or to an analytics service.

Local multiplayer

If you choose local-network multiplayer, the game uses your local network to discover rooms and exchange the nickname, selected character, pet settings, controls and battle state needed by the other participant. A room can be advertised using your device name. This information is shared with the connected participant; it is not sent to a developer-operated matchmaking server. The current connection uses local TCP without application-level encryption. Use a network and participants you trust. You can turn off Local Network access in iOS Settings, or Nearby devices access in Android Settings, and continue playing solo. On Android this permission is not used to determine your location.

Support and backups

If you email pillowpannic@gmail.com, the recipient receives your email address and the information or attachments you send, and uses them to respond to your request. Email is handled by the email providers, including Gmail. Do not include passwords or payment details. Device or cloud backups are controlled by your system settings and may retain copies of local game data.

Your choices

No account registration is required. You can remove game data from this device by deleting the app; offloading the app is not the same as deleting its data. Delete backups separately if you also want to remove those copies. Contact the support address to ask about information you sent by email or request its deletion, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Changes and contact

This policy describes this release. It will be updated if the game's data practices change. Privacy and support contact: pillowpannic@gmail.com.

Текущее соединение работает по локальному TCP без шифрования на уровне приложения. Используйте сеть и соперников, которым доверяете. Доступ к локальной сети можно отозвать в настройках iOS, а доступ к устройствам поблизости — в настройках Android; одиночная игра останется доступной. На Android это разрешение не используется для определения вашего местоположения.
